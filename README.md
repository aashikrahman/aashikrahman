<h1 class="heading-element" dir="auto">🚀 Expertise</h1>

My journey in programming began back in 2010 during my school days, when I first recreated the Google interface using just HTML and CSS. I also built several small applications out of pure curiosity, without even realizing that this spark would later shape my career.

After that early start, I stepped away from coding for almost a decade. But when I returned, everything clicked I realized programming wasn’t just a skill for me, it was my true passion. I restarted from the basics, learned PHP, and began building real applications. That phase reminded me how naturally development comes to me and how deeply I enjoy solving problems through code.

Today, I’m actively working with Node.js, Express, MySQL, and React.js, creating full-stack applications that I personally design, develop, and refine end-to-end. Whether it’s crafting a clean UI, architecting APIs, or optimizing databases, I enjoy every step of the process.

I’m currently working as a Front-End Developer at my company, where I combine my strong UI/UX background with advanced front-end skills to build clean, scalable, and user-focused interfaces. Alongside my professional work, I continue to explore full-stack development, driven purely by passion and curiosity.

With a unique blend of design thinking, technical depth, and consistent self-learning, I love creating meaningful digital experiences. Full-stack development isn’t just my profession it’s something I genuinely enjoy building even for my own ideas and personal projects.
